Purble Place

Play this reconstruction with files from your own Purble Place installation. Your files stay in this browser and are not uploaded.

Select your game files

Pick these files from a Purble Place installation (the three DLLs usually sit next to PurblePlace.exe, and the .mui is in its en-US folder):

FileRequiredFound
or drop the files (or the whole game folder) here

Sound (optional)

The shipped effects are WMA, which only Windows can decode, so the game is silent until it is given a sound pack: one WAV per sound, made once by the desktop build. 

pple-res audio-pack PurblePlace.dll PurblePlace2.dll CardGames.dll --out audio

No sound pack selected; the game will play silently.

Loading the game engine…

Click a building to choose a game, then choose a difficulty. In Purble Pairs, find matching cards. In Comfy Cakes, copy the cake on the TV. In Purble Shop, choose parts and check your guess.
Esc: back to the menu. N: new round. 1 / 2 / 3: change difficulty.
